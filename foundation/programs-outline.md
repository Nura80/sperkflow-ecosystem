# Sperkflow Foundation – Programs Outline

The Sperkflow Foundation offers a range of programs tailored to different stages of personal, professional, and entrepreneurial development. These programs are aligned with our mission to build a thriving ecosystem of innovators, builders, and problem-solvers across Africa.

---

## 1. Sperkflow Spark Club (Primary Tier)

### 🎯 Target Audience
Primary and early secondary school students (ages 8–14).

### 🧭 Program Goals
- Spark early interest in technology, innovation, and problem-solving.
- Encourage creativity, curiosity, and foundational skills in STEM.
- Build a strong pipeline of future digital leaders from underserved communities.

### 🛠️ Program Activities
- Tech and creativity bootcamps.
- Interactive STEM workshops and maker labs.
- "Invent Like Me" story series featuring young role models.
- Monthly quizzes, puzzles, and mini-projects.
- Parental and teacher engagement sessions.

### ⏳ Duration
- 6-week cycles per school term.
- Annual summer camps and competitions.

---

## 2. Sperkflow Young Builders (Secondary Tier)

### 🎯 Target Audience
Senior secondary school students (ages 14–19).

### 🧭 Program Goals
- Equip youth with hands-on skills in coding, robotics, design, and entrepreneurship.
- Promote leadership, teamwork, and community problem-solving.
- Guide students toward purposeful career and academic choices.

### 🛠️ Program Activities
- Code camps and hackathons.
- Digital literacy and financial skills workshops.
- Mentorship with university students and professionals.
- National Science and Innovation Challenge.

### ⏳ Duration
- Semester-based programs (3–4 months).
- Continuous mentorship throughout the year.

---

## 3. Sperkflow Launchpad (University Tier)

### 🎯 Target Audience
Undergraduate students, polytechnic students, and student-led startups.

### 🧭 Program Goals
- Empower students to launch startups or build scalable innovations.
- Provide access to resources, mentorship, and investment opportunities.
- Foster a culture of research, experimentation, and entrepreneurship.

### 🛠️ Program Activities
- Innovation bootcamps and startup weekends.
- Access to the Sperkflow CRM, labs, and incubator.
- Grants, pitch competitions, and demo days.
- Collaboration challenges between students and industries.

### ⏳ Duration
- 6-month cohorts with structured milestones.
- Year-round application and selection windows.

---

## 4. Sperkflow Impact Lab (Graduate Tier)

### 🎯 Target Audience
Young graduates, early professionals, and post-NYSC participants.

### 🧭 Program Goals
- Convert graduate potential into enterprise, research, and tech solutions.
- Equip future founders, researchers, and tech-for-good leaders.
- Provide access to internships, fellowships, and career acceleration.

### 🛠️ Program Activities
- Research and development fellowships.
- Real-world innovation lab projects.
- Startup studio partnerships and incubation.
- Advanced digital and business skills training.

### ⏳ Duration
- 3- to 12-month programs.
- Includes intensive 2-week bootcamp modules.

---

## 5. Sperkflow Catalysts Network (Investors Tier)

### 🎯 Target Audience
Investors, venture capitalists, philanthropists, ecosystem enablers.

### 🧭 Program Goals
- Foster collaboration between funders and grassroots innovators.
- Promote impact investment in African ideas and ventures.
- Create a global alliance of supporters, mentors, and accelerators.

### 🛠️ Program Activities
- Investor roundtables and networking dinners.
- Founder-investor matchmaking events.
- Portfolio reviews and investor showcases.
- Research, policy, and thought leadership initiatives.

### ⏳ Duration
- Ongoing participation.
- Flagship annual Catalyst Forum.

---

## Cross-Cutting Enablers

Each program is powered by shared values and enablers across the foundation:
- 🌍 Inclusion and accessibility
- 💡 Innovation-first thinking
