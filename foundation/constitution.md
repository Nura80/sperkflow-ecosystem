## **Sperkflow Foundation Constitution**

### **1. Name of the Organization**

The name of the organization shall be **Sperkflow Foundation** (hereinafter referred to as "the Foundation").

---

### **2. Mission and Vision**

**Mission:**
To empower innovators, startups, and local communities across Africa by creating inclusive access to knowledge, funding, and structured growth through education, collaboration, and digital innovation hubs.

**Vision:**
To build a future where every African youth can explore their potential, build scalable solutions, and thrive in a connected, equitable innovation ecosystem.

---

### **3. Objectives**

* To run innovation clubs and leadership programs across educational institutions.
* To incubate grassroots ideas and provide technical and business mentorship.
* To connect startups, companies, and investors through the Sperkflow Launchpad.
* To facilitate investment opportunities via the Sperkflow Investors Club.
* To promote digital literacy, ethical technology, and community-based innovation.

---

### **4. Membership**

**4.1. Eligibility:**
Membership is open to individuals, institutions, and organizations who align with the Foundation’s mission. This includes students, founders, professionals, volunteers, educators, and impact investors.

**4.2. Categories:**

1. **General Members** – Active participants of local Sperkflow Clubs.
2. **Core Members** – Volunteers and team leaders driving programs.
3. **Partners** – NGOs, public institutions, and corporate collaborators.
4. **Investors** – Strategic stakeholders in the Catalyst Network.
5. **Advisors** – Senior mentors and board-appointed experts.

**4.3. Rights and Responsibilities:**
Members shall:

* Uphold the values and mission of the Foundation.
* Have access to exclusive training, grants, and opportunities.
* Participate in elections (where applicable) and contribute to decision-making.

---

### **5. Governance**

**5.1. Board of Trustees:**
The highest governing body, responsible for strategic direction, oversight, and compliance. Composed of 5–9 members appointed for 3-year renewable terms.

**5.2. Executive Committee:**
Led by an Executive Director and Program Leads, this body manages operations, partnerships, and reporting.

**5.3. Club Chapters:**
Each Sperkflow Club (Spark, Builders, Launchpad, Impact Lab, and Catalyst Network) operates semi-autonomously under Sperkflow’s principles with a local coordinator and student committee.

**5.4. Decision-Making:**

* The Board meets quarterly; decisions require a two-thirds majority.
* General Assemblies may be called annually or biannually.
* Emergency decisions may be delegated to the Executive Committee.

---

### **6. Finances**

* All funds shall be used solely for the objectives of the Foundation.
* Sources of funds include grants, donations, membership fees, partnerships, and investment programs.
* An annual financial report shall be presented and audited.

---

### **7. Amendments**

This constitution may be amended:

* By a **two-thirds majority** of the Board of Trustees.
* Upon **consultation with core members** and advisory committees.
* Proposed changes must be circulated **30 days prior** to a vote.
* Amendments can be made **once per calendar year**, unless in emergency.

---

### **8. Dissolution**

In the event of dissolution:

* Assets shall be transferred to another non-profit with similar aims.
* The decision must be approved by at least 75% of the Board.
