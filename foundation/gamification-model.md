# 📈 Sperkflow Foundation Gamification Model

## 🎯 Purpose

The Sperkflow Gamification Model is designed to **motivate, engage, and reward** participants across all foundation programs—from young builders to startup founders—by integrating game design principles into educational, mentoring, and entrepreneurial activities.

---

## 🔑 Core Objectives

1. **Increase user participation** across all programs and events.
2. **Encourage long-term commitment** to learning and innovation.
3. **Recognize and reward contributions** at all levels.
4. **Foster community, collaboration, and healthy competition.**
5. **Create a seamless bridge to the Sperkflow for-profit platform.**

---

## 👥 Target Participants

* **Students** (Primary to Tertiary)
* **Aspiring Entrepreneurs**
* **Mentors & Experts**
* **Investors & Partners**
* **Community Volunteers**

---

## 🧩 Badges, Levels, and Skill Trees

**Badges** represent progress and specialization in specific domains:

* **🔥 Spark Initiate** – First activity completed
* **🧠 Knowledge Seeker** – Completed 3+ learning modules
* **🤝 Mentor Badge** – Mentored 5+ participants
* **🚀 Founder Badge** – Submitted a verified MVP
* **🌟 Super Contributor** – Top 10% of leaderboard
* **🌍 Impact Champion** - Completed an "Impact Quest"

**Levels** indicate your growth over time:

* Level 1: 0–199 points
* Level 2: 200–499 points
* Level 3: 500–999 points
* Level 4: 1000–1499 points
* Level 5: 1500+ points

**Skill Trees** provide a personalized learning journey. Users can choose a skill to develop (e.g., "Entrepreneurship," "Web Development," "Design Thinking"), and the skill tree will guide them through a series of courses, projects, and mentorship sessions to master that skill.

## 🔄 Redemption & Impact Integration

Points can be **redeemed** or **translated into opportunities**, including:

* Free access to paid events
* Featured project promotion
* Early access to funding rounds
* Startup validation support
* Partner internships or fellowships

### **Sperkflow Marketplace**

The Sperkflow Marketplace serves as a bridge to the for-profit Sperkflow platform. Users can redeem their points for premium rewards, such as:

* A free month of "pro" access on the Sperkflow platform
* A featured spot on the platform's homepage
* A guaranteed introduction to a venture capitalist

## 🎯 Alignment with Mission

This gamification model is not just about fun—it is a **strategic tool** to drive:

* Innovation and learning

* Scalable social impact

It ensures that **every small action adds up to a larger purpose**: building a future-ready Africa through knowledge, innovation, and opportunity.
