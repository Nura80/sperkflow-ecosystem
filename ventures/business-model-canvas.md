# **Sperkflow Ventures Ltd – Business Model Canvas**

## 🔹 1. **Customer Segments**

*   Student innovators and university-based founders
*   Early-stage and grassroots entrepreneurs
*   Startup teams in emerging markets
*   Development agencies, NGOs, and public innovation programs
*   Investors seeking early pipeline deals
*   Incubators and accelerators in Africa needing co-building support
*   Corporate Innovation Departments

---

## 🔹 2. **Value Propositions**

*   Venture-building support from idea to launch
*   Access to a trusted product studio and builder network
*   Innovation frameworks tailored to African markets
*   Community-based validation and local traction models
*   Startup storytelling and branding support
*   Lean startup coaching, MVP prototyping, and pitch readiness
*   Gateway to investor and partner connections
*   Access to a curated talent pool

---

## 🔹 3. **Channels**

*   Sperkflow Launchpad and partner university programs
*   Social media (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Telegram)
*   Online hackathons, demo days, and pitch events
*   Collaborations with tech hubs and student unions
*   Referrals via Sperkflow mentors and community
*   Email newsletters and venture reports

---

## 🔹 4. **Customer Relationships**

*   Peer-led support communities and cohorts
*   Personalized venture advisor check-ins
*   Telegram/Slack groups for active ventures
*   Feedback loops during validation, MVP, and scale-up stages
*   Hybrid virtual + in-person coaching
*   Access to shared founder resources and templates

---

## 🔹 5. **Revenue Streams**

*   Startup equity in co-built ventures
*   Venture studio service fees (e.g., branding, tech prototyping)
*   Innovation partnership contracts with NGOs or institutions
*   Program management for accelerators/incubators
*   Referral bonuses from investors and funders
*   Branded reports, playbooks, or events (future)
*   Data and Insights

---

## 🔹 6. **Key Resources**

*   Sperkflow team (core + fractional experts)
*   Builder and mentor network (tech, business, design)
*   University and grassroots partner networks
*   Project and venture management systems
*   Digital infrastructure (site, CRM, content systems)
*   Templates, venture playbooks, and IP (in development)

---

## 🔹 7. **Key Activities**

*   Identifying and selecting promising venture ideas
*   Supporting MVP and lean validation
*   Building branding and product assets
*   Managing partnerships and collaboration projects
*   Guiding founders through venture acceleration
*   Maintaining community engagement
*   Preparing ventures for fundraising

---

## 🔹 8. **Key Partnerships**

*   Universities and student associations
*   Local hubs, labs, and accelerators
*   Technical experts and freelancers
*   NGO and development agencies
*   Early-stage angel investors
*   Legal, branding, and accounting partners
*   Mentors, advisors, and domain specialists

---

## 🔹 9. **Cost Structure**

*   Staff and expert contributions (full/fractional)
*   Product studio services (design, dev, testing)
*   Venture management and platform maintenance
*   Marketing and community engagement
*   Program delivery (events, coaching, partnerships)
*   Legal, compliance, and accounting overheads
